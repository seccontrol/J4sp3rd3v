**SecControl** is an Italian cybersecurity intelligence unit and perimetric attack surface defense hub based in Milan, Italy. We provide real-time external surface reconnaissance, automated vulnerability detection, and open-source endpoint monitoring for enterprises and institutions.

---

## 🚀 Featured Open-Source Repositories

### 🛡️ [seccontrol-sentinel](https://github.com/J4sp3rd3v/seccontrol-sentinel)
> **Open-Source Endpoint Security, Behavioral Antivirus & Perimeter Monitoring Watchdog**
* **File Integrity Monitoring (FIM)**: Real-time cryptographic tracking (SHA-256) of system binaries and web roots.
* **Behavioral Antivirus & Ransomware Guard**: Immediate mitigation against suspicious encryption anomalies, reverse shells, and unauthorized processes.
* **Perimeter Exposure Auditor**: Automated auditing for cleartext protocols (RFC 959 FTP 21, Telnet), missing DMARC/SPF email spoofing protections, and missing TLS/HSTS.
* **NIS2 Compliance Engine**: Generates turnkey technical posture reports aligned with **Art. 21 D.Lgs. 138/2024**.

### 🌐 [seccontrol](https://github.com/J4sp3rd3v/seccontrol)
> **SecControl Global Hack Map & Attack Surface Management Platform**
* Interactive 3D threat map tracking active exploited vulnerabilities (CISA KEV), perimetric disclosures, and European security telemetry.
* Automated incident response runbooks and enterprise risk scoring.

---

## 📊 Core Focus & Capabilities

* **External Attack Surface Management (EASM)**: Passive perimetric auditing without intrusive scans.
* **Continuous Threat Intelligence**: Real-time aggregation of CERT-AgID, CISA, NVD, and global cyber threat telemetry.
* **Compliance & Advisory**: Tailored remediation runbooks compliant with European NIS2 directives.
